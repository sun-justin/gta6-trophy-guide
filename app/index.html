Roadmap to the platinum

Work in this order. Story trophies come first because several are tied to a single mission — do them on your first pass. Collectibles and cleanup come last, when the whole map is open to you. The platinum, “Vice Got a Price!”, unlocks automatically once the other 54 are earned.

    What “missable” means here

    A trophy is flagged missable when its description ties it to one specific story mission or one-time moment. Whether GTA VI lets you replay missions is not yet confirmed, so do those on your first pass through the named mission. Chapter-completion trophies are not flagged — finishing a chapter cannot be missed.

    Your progress

    Ticking a trophy saves it in this browser only (localStorage). Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; Import loads such a file back. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the guide fetches nothing from the network.
